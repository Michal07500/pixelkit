## TikTok specifics

- **Account:** switch to a **Business account** (Settings → Account → Switch to Business). Business accounts can add a website link to the profile without waiting for 1,000 followers in most regions. If yours can't yet, write "free lesson → link on my Instagram @…" in the bio.
- **Upload from a computer** (tiktok.com/upload) for the best quality, and schedule posts up to 10 days ahead there.
- **Sound:** our phonk beat is our own audio, so the version with music is safe. Want a trending sound? Upload the `-nomusic` version and add the sound in the TikTok editor at about 20–30 % volume.
- **First 3 comments:** reply to every comment in the first hour. TikTok pushes videos with active comment sections.
- **Pin** the Horror Pack and free-lesson videos on your profile.

## YouTube Shorts specifics

- YouTube app → **+ → Create a Short → Upload** (or studio.youtube.com on a computer).
- **Title** = the hook (under 60 characters), **description** = the caption plus your website link. Links in Shorts descriptions are clickable.
- Upload the 16:9 trailer (`ad-trailer-16x9.mp4`) as a normal video and the course **free lesson video** as a normal video too. Long videos build trust; Shorts bring reach.
