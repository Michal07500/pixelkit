FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need coding experience?

No. We start with installing Roblox Studio and placing your first part. Scripting is introduced step by step, and every line is explained.

What hardware do I need?

A Windows PC or Mac that can run Roblox Studio, which is free. You can't build on a phone or tablet, but you can playtest your game there.

When does the course launch?

We're in final production. Join the list to get the free lesson now and early-access pricing on launch day.

Will I make money from my game?

You'll learn exactly how Roblox monetization works and how to set it up properly. Earnings depend on how many people play your game, so nobody can honestly guarantee income. We'll show you what actually moves the needle.

What's the difference between the course and Genre Packs?

The Core Course teaches the fundamentals while you build one complete game. Genre Packs are shorter, focused courses for a specific genre, each with ready-to-use systems you adapt to your own project.

Is this an official Roblox course?

No. PIXEL KIT is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Roblox Corporation.
