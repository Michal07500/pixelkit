## Email 2 · Day 1

**Subject:** Did your intro screen work?
**Preview:** The 3 mistakes almost everyone makes

Hey,

quick check: did your intro screen show up?

If not, it's almost always one of these:

1. **The script is in the wrong place.** It must be a *LocalScript* inside *ReplicatedFirst*, not a Script, not in Workspace.
2. **The logo is still in moderation.** Freshly uploaded images take a few minutes before they show up in-game.
3. **The asset ID is wrong.** It should look like `rbxassetid://1234567890`, with no spaces.

Still stuck? Reply with a screenshot of your Explorer window and I'll take a look.

And if it worked: congrats. You just wrote code that runs on every device your players use. That's the same skill the biggest Roblox games are built on.
