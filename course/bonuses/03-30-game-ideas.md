# Bonus · 30 Roblox Game Ideas

Stuck on what to build after Coin Rush? Here are 30 ideas, each with its core loop, the course systems it reuses, and a fair way to monetize it. They're starting points, not guarantees: what a game earns depends on how fun it is and how well you market it.

**You'll get:** 30 buildable concepts, mapped to the skills you already have.

---

## How to pick

1. **Pick a genre you love playing.** You'll spend weeks inside it.
2. **Start small.** Choose an idea you can ship in 2–4 weeks, then grow it with updates.
3. **Twist one thing.** A familiar genre plus one fresh idea beats something completely unfamiliar.

Systems key: **R** rounds & timers (M7) · **D** saved data (M6) · **S** secure remotes (M5) · **U** UI/HUD (M4) · **$** store (M8)
