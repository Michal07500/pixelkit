# Bonus · Game Launch Checklist

Print it, tick it off, launch with confidence. Every item here is covered in the course; this is the version you keep on your desk.

**You'll get:** a step-by-step list from "almost done" to "live and growing".

---

## 1. Before you publish

### Gameplay
- [ ] A new player understands what to do within 30 seconds, without reading anything
- [ ] The core loop is fun for 10 minutes with zero upgrades
- [ ] Every hazard is visibly dangerous (color, glow, sound)
- [ ] You tested a full session with at least 2 players (**Test → Clients and Servers**)

### Technical
- [ ] No errors in **Output** during a full playtest
- [ ] Every RemoteEvent validates types, values, state and rate
- [ ] Data saves on leave, on autosave and on shutdown (`BindToClose`)
- [ ] Data loading failures kick with a message instead of giving a blank profile
- [ ] Developer products are granted only in `ProcessReceipt`, with receipt records
- [ ] No unknown scripts from the Toolbox (search all scripts for `require(`)

### Devices
- [ ] UI checked in the device emulator: phone, tablet, desktop, console
- [ ] Buttons are big enough to tap (at least ~44 px on a phone)
- [ ] Tested on a real phone from your own account

## 2. Your game page

- [ ] Icon: one clear subject, bold colors, readable at tiny size, little or no text
- [ ] 3+ thumbnails showing real gameplay moments with short captions
- [ ] Title: short and clear about the genre
- [ ] Description: first two lines say what you do and why it's fun
- [ ] Genre, supported devices and maturity questionnaire filled in
- [ ] Server size set (8–12 for most round-based games)

## 3. Launch day

- [ ] Set the experience to **Public**
- [ ] Play it yourself from phone and PC one last time
- [ ] Post a short clip (15–30 s) of the best moment on TikTok, Reels and Shorts
- [ ] Share it in your Roblox community group and Discord
- [ ] Ask 5 friends to play, and watch them play without helping

## 4. First week

- [ ] Check **Analytics** daily: day-1 retention, session length, where players leave
- [ ] Log onboarding funnel steps and find the step with the biggest drop
- [ ] Fix the #1 drop-off before adding anything new
- [ ] Reply to every comment and bug report
- [ ] Ship a small update with a changelog by day 7

## 5. Growing

- [ ] Update every 1–2 weeks (new map, upgrade, event, or code)
- [ ] Run an **icon experiment** with 2–3 variants
- [ ] One short-form clip per day from real gameplay
- [ ] Only run Roblox Ads once day-1 retention is healthy
- [ ] Change one price at a time and compare a week before vs. after

> **Watch out:** Don't judge your game by day 1. Most games that grow do it through steady updates over weeks, not a lucky first day.
